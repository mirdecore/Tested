Ordering & Payment

  • Add products to your cart (or tap Buy Now), fill in your delivery details and tap Place Order on WhatsApp.
  • WhatsApp opens with your order and address — just press Send.
  • We reply with payment details. All orders are prepaid via UPI or bank transfer.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) is not available.

Order Updates

After payment we confirm your order on WhatsApp and share dispatch and tracking details when your order ships. Please keep your order ID (for example MD261115-AB12) handy when you contact us.

Damaged or Wrong Item?

Please message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with your order ID, photos and an unboxing video. See our Terms & Conditions for details.
