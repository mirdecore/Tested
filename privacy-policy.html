Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mir Decor ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information when you visit or order from this website. By using the website you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Order & delivery details you enter at checkout: name, mobile number, email (optional), address, landmark, city, state and pincode.
  • Order details: products, quantities, prices and order ID.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • Basic usage data (such as pages visited and device/browser type) collected through Google Firebase Analytics.

We do not collect or store card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords on this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, confirm, pack and deliver your order.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp or phone about your order, payment and delivery.
  • To provide customer support and handle returns or complaints.
  • To improve our website, products and service.

3. Where Your Information Is Stored

  • On your device: your cart, wishlist and delivery details are saved in your browser so you can order faster next time. You can clear them anytime by clearing your browser data.
  • With our service providers: orders are stored securely in Google Firebase, and order messages are sent through WhatsApp. These services have their own privacy policies.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with people who need it to serve you — for example courier partners for delivery — or when required by law.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

The website uses browser storage to remember your cart, wishlist and details, and analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. You can block or clear these in your browser settings; some features may not work without them.

6. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. We keep order records only as long as needed for orders, support, accounting and legal purposes.

7. Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by contacting us at +91 78198 86014 or on WhatsApp.

8. Children

This website is not intended for children under 18. Orders should be placed by adults.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the updated date.
