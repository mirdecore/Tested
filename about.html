About Us

Genuine handcrafted metalwork, straight from the artisans of Aligarh.

Mir Decor is a home-décor brand from Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh — a city whose workshops have shaped metal for generations. We create handcrafted metal wall shelves, planters, planter stands and décor items that bring warmth, strength and character into modern Indian homes.

Every Mir Decor piece is made by hand. Our artisans cut, bend, weld, polish and finish each product in small batches, and every piece is checked before it is packed. Because it is handmade, no two pieces are ever exactly alike — and that is exactly what makes them special.

Our Story

Aligarh is famous across India for its metal craft. Yet many of the skilled hands behind that craft rarely get the recognition — or the fair price — their work deserves. Mir Decor was started to change that: to take genuine metalwork straight from the artisans of Aligarh to homes across India, without the long chain of middlemen.

Today we design and make shelves for living rooms and kitchens, planters for balconies and gardens, and décor accents that turn an empty wall or corner into the best part of the room.

Our Mission

Honour the Craft

Keep the metal craft of Aligarh alive by giving local artisans steady work, fair pay and the pride of seeing their work in homes across India.

Built to Last

Make décor that is strong, safe and beautiful for years — not throw-away pieces that lose their shine in a season.

Honest & Personal

Fair prices, clear policies and real people on WhatsApp who help you before and after you buy.
